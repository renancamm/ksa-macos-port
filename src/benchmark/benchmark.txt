# The benchmark: one session of the game, in sections. See director/Benchmark.cs for the format and
# director/Directives.cs for the @ directives; other commands are the game's console commands.
#
# The tour of the worlds is one flight of the camera (director/Path.cs): it passes through each @key at its time,
# turning, climbing and turning again as it goes, from Earth to Jupiter without a cut. Every @shot is a checkpoint: the
# camera and the simulation ease to a stop, the game catches up with the script's game time and the screenshot is
# taken, so the screenshots show the same moments on every Mac. benchmark.sh --explore captures keys from the game.
#
# Fast mode (benchmark.sh --fast) plays a short test of its own, the lineup section (the last below): everything set up
# at one moment of the game, then the views with the game paused. What a run plays when no places are named, in fast
# mode and in the cinematic tour (benchmark.sh and the game's Mac Port tab read these two lines):
fast lineup
tour earth moon mars saturn jupiter capsule launch giga

# Stress: the bundled "giga rocket" (about 2,000 parts: 200 solid boosters on eight curved arms, hundreds of batteries
# and lights) on launch pad OLM A at Starbase. The camera turns slowly around it all along, starting close and slowly
# rising and pulling back: the pad, the ignition and lift-off (200 plumes, many lights, a spiral of smoke), the rocket
# blown up whole, the fire and smoke seen from above. No checkpoint between the ignition and the explosion: the
# simulation slowing down or stopping makes the game's g-load check break the rocket up at a moment that differs from
# run to run.

# Lineup, fast mode's test: the vehicles' views and six of Earth's places at one moment of the game, the game paused.
# The camera stays on a kitten in its helmet floating in orbit, looking into it with Earth going by behind, while
# everything is set up (@kitten, @fly; @front holds the camera on it): each view has a vehicle of its own, placed and
# started so that its moment comes at the same game time: the giga rocket blown up whole as it lights its 200 boosters
# on its pad at Starbase, a rocket rising from LC-39A, one high over the coast from Vandenberg (put in the air with
# @fly, so it skips its climb), and a capsule's cockpit in orbit by the kitten, the sun coming in through one window
# onto its wall and Earth in the other (@fly, @face, @fov; the patch of sunlight needs IVA ray tracing on). A vehicle's
# throttle starts at full, so a rocket starts with its engines and the staging key alone. Then the game pauses and the
