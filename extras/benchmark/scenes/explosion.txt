# Explosion: the bundled rocket blown up on launch pad LC-39A at dusk, seen from 150 m away: the fireball, debris
# and the smoke volumes that follow.
start Sandbox

0 simspeed 0
0.5 showorbit all false
1 @launch benchmark-srb at CCSFS LC-39A
3 follow Earth
3.5 camera Free
4 goto 28.6072 -80.6056
4.5 altitude 50 m
5 @lookat 28.6083 -80.6041
8 simspeed 1
10 @measure pad 3
13 explode benchmark-srb
13 @measure blast 3
13.8 @shot blast
16 @measure smoke 8
20 @shot smoke
24 @end
