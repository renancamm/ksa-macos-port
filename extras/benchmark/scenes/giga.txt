# Many parts: the bundled "giga rocket" (about 2,000 parts: 200 solid boosters on eight curved arms, hundreds of
# batteries and lights) on launch pad LC-39A, seen from above with the camera turning around it: first with the
# simulation paused (rendering only), then running on the pad (physics and part updates on the CPU), then staged,
# ignited and lifting off (200 plumes, many lights). About 15 s into the flight the g-load breaks it up.
