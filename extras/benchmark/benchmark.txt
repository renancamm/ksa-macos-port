# The benchmark: one session of the game, in sections. See mod/Benchmark.cs for the format and mod/Directives.cs for
# the @ directives; other commands are the game's console commands.
#
# The tour of the worlds is one flight of the camera (mod/Path.cs): it passes through each @key at its time, turning,
# climbing and turning again as it goes, from Earth to Jupiter without a cut. Every @shot is a checkpoint: the camera
# and the simulation ease to a stop, the game catches up with the script's game time and the screenshot is taken, so
# the screenshots show the same moments on every Mac. benchmark.sh --explore captures keys from the game.

# Stress: the bundled "giga rocket" (about 2,000 parts: 200 solid boosters on eight curved arms, hundreds of batteries
